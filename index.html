03 / agentic ai

Agentic AI

I see agentic AI as a practical opportunity to extend automation beyond rigid, predefined workflows. By combining AI agents with deterministic systems, APIs, business rules, and existing enterprise software, it becomes possible to automate processes that previously required too much interpretation, coordination, or manual intervention.

The most valuable solutions are rarely fully autonomous. They use agents where flexibility and reasoning are useful, while keeping critical operations controlled, predictable, and auditable. I am particularly interested in identifying business processes where this combination can reduce repetitive work, connect fragmented systems, and create new forms of intelligent automation.

I am always open to discussing where agentic AI can create measurable value for a business, and how it can be integrated into existing processes in a sensible, secure, and maintainable way.
